// ABOUT ME

Building interactive AR experiences, web applications, and automated systems.

I'm Ariel Daud, an AR TikTok Developer & Creative Technologist from Indonesia.

My journey in AR began in 2019 as an Instagram Filter Creator using Spark AR, actively creating interactive effects until January 2025 when Meta officially sunsetted the platform. Since 2022, I have been an early adopter of TikTok Effect House starting from its beta release. To this day, I continue actively developing interactive TikTok game filters and delivering high-impact AR campaigns for various leading brands.

Winning 1st Place in the Spark AR Competition (2021) deepened my focus on real-time interactive game mechanics, head-tracking experiences, and visual scripting logic. Alongside AR, I connect my creative technology with full-stack web development (Laravel, PHP, Next.js), Linux server management, and workflow automation.

Today, I work across the entire product cycle: designing the AR interaction, crafting visual scripting mechanics, automating backend workflows, and delivering high-converting brand filter campaigns.
